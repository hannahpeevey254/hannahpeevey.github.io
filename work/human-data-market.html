ARTIST
STATEMENT

Intimacy has become infrastructure. What we say about ourselves no longer stays self-expression; it becomes a data layer other systems run on. My research sits at the threshold where a chosen vulnerability turns into an extracted one, and The Human Data Market stages that moment as a stock exchange of confessions, with cravings, weaknesses and desires priced in real time. Participation here is conscious: a mediated intimacy in which we negotiate the terms of our own exposure. By putting a price on our own data, we reclaim some authority over it, meeting the systems that read us as products as participants instead.

01 — Concept

WHAT YOU WALKED INTO

A booth that buys your intimate data. You answer six quick questions. Each answer is sold to a buyer. You get a number, a receipt and a price.

The people who own the platforms see our most private thoughts as a number. This makes that number visible, and starts the conversation.

WHAT IS MIRA

MIRA is a digital arts and contemporary music festival held every year in Barcelona. This was the 15th edition, at Fira Montjuïc, on 6 and 7 November 2026.

04 — Buyers

WHO PAYS, AND HOW MUCH

Six buyers, ranked by what a single signal is worth to them. Each question has three answers and three different buyers. Each buyer appears exactly three times across the six questions.
